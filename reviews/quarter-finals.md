## 🔮 Podium Scenarios — where they'll finish

With four teams left there are exactly **16 ways** the semis, 3rd-place play-off and final can fall. Here's how the podium shakes out across all of them:

- **Peter Hick** — the runaway leader. On the podium in **all 16 scenarios** and finishes **1st in 11 of them** (10 outright). The only way he loses top spot is if England lose the semi to Argentina *and* France or Spain go on to lift the trophy.
- **Sam Parke** — the dark horse, and the reason is his £3 punt on DR Congo (17 points banked from a group-stage draw, a win, and a Round-of-32 run). Holding **both** Spain and Argentina, he finishes **1st in 5 scenarios** (2 outright) and podiums in 8. He needs Argentina to beat England in the semi.
- **Sunil Kumar** — 1st in **2 scenarios** (both shared with Sam), on the podium in 8. He needs Spain to go all the way.
- **Elliott Wilson** — 1st in **1 scenario** (outright), podiums in 6. Needs France to win it *and* England to lose their semi.
- **Brad Vaile** — podium in 4 scenarios (needs France to win).
- **Matt Peel** — podium in 4 scenarios (his England the key).
- **Clare Daniel & James Buxton** — podium in 2 scenarios each (both need Argentina to win it all).

The short version: **Peter Hick is nailed on for a podium and just needs England to reach the final to seal top spot**, while Sam Parke's twin ownership of Spain and Argentina makes him the man nobody wants to see in the final.
