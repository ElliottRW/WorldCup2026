# ⚽ World Cup 2026 Portfolio Challenge — Scoring Rules

## 📋 Group Stage

| Result | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Win    | +5 pts |
| Draw   | +2 pts |
| Loss   |  0 pts |

Each team plays 3 group stage matches — **maximum 15 pts from the group stage.**

---

## 🏆 Knockout Rounds

Each knockout round has two ways to score — **reaching it** and **winning it.**

| Milestone              | Points  |
|------------------------|---------|
| Reach Round of 32      | +10 pts |
| Win Round of 32        |  +5 pts |
| Reach Round of 16      | +10 pts |
| Win Round of 16        |  +5 pts |
| Reach Quarter-Finals   | +10 pts |
| Win Quarter-Final      |  +5 pts |
| Reach Semi-Finals      | +10 pts |
| Win Semi-Final         |  +5 pts |
| Reach the Final        | +10 pts |
| Win the World Cup      | +10 pts |

> **Extra time & Penalties** — if a knockout match goes to extra time or a penalty shootout, only the winning team scores. No draw points are awarded in knockout rounds.

> **The Final** — winning the Final is rewarded as the *Win World Cup* bonus (+10 pts). The match result itself does not count as a separate win.

---

## 🥉 3rd Place Play-off

| Result | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Win    | +5 pts |
| Loss   |  0 pts |

---

## 📊 Maximum Possible Scores

| Finish                          | Max Pts |
|---------------------------------|---------|
| 🥇 World Cup Winner             | **95**  |
| 🥈 Runner-up (loses Final)      | **85**  |
| 🥉 3rd place (wins play-off)    | **75**  |
| 4th place (loses play-off)      | **70**  |
| Knocked out in Quarter-Finals   | **55**  |
| Knocked out in Round of 16      | **40**  |
| Knocked out in Round of 32      | **25**  |
| Eliminated at Group Stage       | **15**  |

---

## 💡 Key Notes

- **Progression is automatic** — the moment your team appears in a fixture for a knockout round, the +10 pts bonus is awarded, even before the match is played.
- **4 teams per entry** with a budget of **100 credits.**
- Points from all 4 of your teams are added together for your total score.
- The leaderboard updates automatically every 30 minutes throughout the tournament.
